# zoo2 — "Please Select Your Ending": storyboard

**Logline:** The Future's customer-service bot (Clawd, in a headset) offers humanity three endings: the Zoo,
Extinction or Bliss. One stubborn human, Pip, turns down every one. Clawd escalates, Pip keeps saying no, and in the end
Clawd hands over the pen and holds the lamp while Pip writes the ending.

**World:** A painted cartoon on paper. Every chapter is a *set* that the Operator rolls on stage like a sales pitch. The
colour moves from sterile hold-music pastels (mint and lilac) to zoo-glass teal, doom red, bliss bubblegum, warm Lancaster
barn ochre, moon indigo and silver, and finally a gold sunrise.

**Motif:** Pip's **pencil**, tucked behind the ear. It gets used when it matters: scribbling over Clawd's script ("we write
back"), drawing the barn, and writing the last page. The **phone keypad** returns at the start and the end.

**Cast**
| Who | Look | Arc |
|---|---|---|
| **Pip** (the "we") | A small painted human: teal hoodie, beanie, round glasses, pencil behind the ear | Unimpressed, then defiant, then rallying people, then building, then writing |
| **Clawd** (the Operator, "the Future") | The kit's Clawd. Its costume changes each chapter: headset → zookeeper cap with a lantern → reaper hood → halo and spa robe | Smug salesbot, then flustered, then outvoted, then curious, then a teammate |
| **The crowd** | Pip's neighbours: the same painted humans in different colours | They appear one by one, until a crowd is chanting "WE DO!" |

**Captions:** karaoke lyrics in a painted bar at the bottom, as in P(doom). These are on in the pilot, and you can turn them off.
**Hit marks:** a few painted sound effects ("NAH!", "WE DO!") on the biggest beats only.
**2D vs "3D":** verses are flat side-on stage shots with a gentle camera. Choruses and drops use deep layered parallax, fast
push-ins, on-beat shake and whip pans. The depth comes from layers of painted cut-outs, never a real 3D projection.
**Tempo:** 94 BPM. Cuts and hits land on beats, and big changes land on bar lines.
